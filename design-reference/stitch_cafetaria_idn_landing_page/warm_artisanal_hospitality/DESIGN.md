## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern Indonesian hospitality through an artisanal culinary lens. The aesthetic captures the sensory ritual of single-origin coffee brewing, natural timber finishes, and warm tropical sunlight filtering into high-ceilinged cafe interiors.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Welcoming, unhurried, and grounded in authentic Indonesian warmth (*keramahan*).
- **Artisanal Precision:** Thoughtfully crafted, honoring regional coffee origins (Aceh Gayo, Toraja, Kintamani) with modern culinary restraint.
- **Refined Contemporary:** Sophisticated yet approachable; luxurious without feeling sterile or exclusive.

### Design Movement
A blend of **Contemporary Organic Modernism** and **Tactile Minimalism**. Interfaces prioritize creamy negative space, deep espresso structure, glowing amber focal points, and soft organic rounding. Visuals avoid clinical gloss, instead favoring tactile matte surfaces, muted earth dividers, and rich appetizing contrast.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the roasting and brewing process: glowing amber crema, rich roasted espresso beans, steaming milk froth, and weathered volcanic stone roastery counters.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C27803` - Amber Crema):** Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, interactive accents, and highlight tags. Evokes warm poured caramel and fresh extraction.
- **Secondary (`#2B1810` - Deep Espresso):** Anchors high-emphasis headers, prominent brand surfaces, dark state elements, and hero containers.
- **Tertiary (`#E5E0D8` - Muted Stone Gray):** Subtle component outlines, structural dividers, chip surfaces, and muted neutral badges.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Cream Froth):** The core background foundation, replacing harsh pure whites with a gentle, milky warmth that reduces eye fatigue and enhances photography.
- **Text Charcoal (`#1C1917`):** Primary body and interface typography, providing strict WCAG AAA contrast against cream surfaces while harmonizing with deep espresso elements.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a native Indonesian-designed modern sans-serif celebrated for its balanced geometric rhythm, soft circular counters, and legible clarity.

### Typographic Intent
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to deliver an editorial coffee table book poise. Heavy weights anchor menus and bean origin profiles with culinary prestige.
- **Body & Editorial:** Optimized for leisurely browsing with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) that preserve breathability across multi-paragraph tasting notes and menu descriptions.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with medium to semi-bold weights and slight positive tracking to ensure fast legibility in fast-paced ordering flows and nutrition metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built upon a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, reflowing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** Max container width of `1280px` centered with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Food and coffee imagery must always be paired with generous padding (`space-xl` to `space-2xl` on section breaks), honoring the unhurried cafe atmosphere.
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) is strictly reserved for tight metadata pairings, such as origin altitude pills and flavor profile badges.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, blue-tinted digital drop shadows. Instead, it employs **Tonal Warmth** and **Ambient Sun-Cast Shadows** tinted with espresso and amber undertones (`rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.06)`).

### Elevation Layers
1. **Base Surface (0dp):** Pure cream froth `#FAF7F2`.
2. **Elevated Surface / Cards (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#FAF7F2` with a subtle outline of `#E5E0D8` (1px solid) and a diffused warm glow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Hover (Level 2):** Elevated menu items, cart drawers, and active order tiles lift subtly: `0px 8px 24px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(194, 120, 3, 0.04)`.
4. **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Deep dialogs and tasting cards cast: `0px 16px 40px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.14)`. Backdrop scrims use `#2B1810` at 40% opacity with a soft `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is anchored at **Level 2 (Rounded)**, offering friendly, tactile contours that reflect handcrafted ceramic drinkware and wooden tray edges.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Input fields, interactive chips, selection controls, and inner media thumbnails.
- **Large (`1rem` / `16px`):** Food and coffee menu cards, modal sheets, banner containers, and checkout drawers.
- **Extra Large (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Featured artisanal showcase containers and hero imagery masks.
- **Pill / Circular (`9999px`):** Action buttons, status tags, brewing indicator dots, and quantity steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#C27803`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover transitions smoothly to deeper caramel `#A36302`.
- **Secondary / Roastery Dark:** Background `#2B1810`, text `#FAF7F2`, rounded pill shape. Used for upscale checkout and subscription actions.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Background transparent, 1.5px border `#E5E0D8`, text `#1C1917`. On hover: border `#C27803` and text `#C27803`.

### Cards & Menu Containers
- Surface is `#FFFFFF` resting over `#FAF7F2`, bordered by 1px solid `#E5E0D8` with `rounded-lg` (`16px`).
- Food and bean photography uses top-flush framing with an inner soft gradient scrim to ensure title legibility when text overlays imagery.

### Chips & Tasting Note Badges
- Compact indicators (e.g., "Gayo Wash", "Brown Sugar & Floral", "Medium Dark Roast") use background `#FAF7F2` with 1px solid `#E5E0D8`, text `#2B1810`, and standard roundedness (`8px`).
- Active or selected chips transition to background `#C27803` with crisp white text.

### Inputs & Quantity Controls
- Text fields feature background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E5E0D8`, text `#1C1917`, and standard roundedness (`8px`). Focus state illuminates with a 2px ring of `#C27803`.
- Stepper buttons for beverage customization (e.g., ice level, oat milk swap, shot count) use compact `#FAF7F2` pills with espresso icons.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Origin Flavor Bar:** Visual spectrum bar charting sweetness, acidity, body, and aroma using smooth amber progression meters.
- **Roast Profile Meter:** Segmented visual scale ranging from light blonde to dark espresso roast with rounded indicators.
